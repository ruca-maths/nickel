## Claim 8.2 separates survival from local selection

Source: https://arxiv.org/html/1809.03763v2, Claim 8.2(4), HTML 2040.
Write R_delta for the coarse checkerboard union in that claim, D_delta
for the union of the designated fine cells at the matching v, and
A_delta for the actual hole after Construction 6.1 subtracts the
previous exclusion. Claim 8.2(4) explicitly gives
R_delta ∩ D_delta ⊆ A_delta. Thus, conditional on Claim 8.2 with the
matching depth, v, cutoff and finite family for the new index, any
designated fine cell entirely inside R_delta already survives. No
additional disjointness argument for that cell is needed.

The remaining local selection can be stated with C the previously
selected cell: choose a higher-rank theta and a matched designated cell
C' entirely inside C ∩ R_theta, with a companion in the portion of
R_theta required by Proposition 8.4. If the companion is in R_theta and
its G-cell belongs to the B2 complement family, it avoids every
designated G-cell in that block. This gives the required difference
membership once the fine hole union in Proposition 8.4 is identified
with its designated subfamily at that block. The coarse restriction
C ∩ R_theta and the matched companion are still to be established.

There is a specific quantifier check before using (4) uniformly:
8.1(C)(1–4) chooses v beyond e for the finite competing family and
beyond the cutoff q_k. A bound on the size of each competing family
does not bound e across those families. A common checkerboard for
the chosen stages therefore needs either a uniform eventual-separation
threshold obtained before selecting the prefix, or a direct argument
at the fixed v using the actual coordinate values. This check applies
to the attempted reconstruction and does not refute the preprint.
